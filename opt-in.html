How people opt in

By texting this number you agree to receive replies, reminders and alerts from Brent Shores Personal Assistant. Msg frequency varies, typically a few messages a week. Msg & data rates may apply. 1. Texting the number first

Brent gives the person the number above along with this disclosure. Nothing is sent to anyone until they text the number themselves. Texts from numbers Brent doesn't know get no automatic reply; Brent is notified and decides whether to answer. The first reply identifies the sender:

"Hi, this is Brent's assistant. Brent Shores uses this number for his personal assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

2. Verbal consent to Brent

Brent asks in person or by phone, using this script:

"Can my assistant, Brent Shores Personal Assistant, text you at this number, for things like reminders and answering questions when I'm busy? It'll be a few messages a week at most. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP anytime to opt out, or HELP for help. Terms and privacy policy are at bcshores.github.io/haptic-sms."

Brent records the person's name, number and the date they agreed before the assistant sends them any message. Their first message is the same identification and disclosure shown above.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to stop all messages; you will get one confirmation and nothing further. Reply HELP, or email hapticbrent@gmail.com, for help. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
