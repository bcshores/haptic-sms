How people opt in

Recipients are Brent Shores's family members and friends. Nobody is added from a list, and consent is never asked for by text message. There are two ways to opt in:

1. Texting the number first

A person who sends a text to the assistant's number has started the conversation. The first reply identifies the sender:

"Hi, this is Brent's assistant. Brent Shores uses this number for his personal assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

2. Verbal consent to Brent

Brent asks in person or by phone, using this script:

"Can my assistant text you at this number, for things like reminders and answering questions when I'm busy? It'll be a few messages a week at most. Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime to stop. Terms and privacy policy are at bcshores.github.io/haptic-sms."

Brent records the person's name, number and the date they agreed before the assistant sends them any message.
