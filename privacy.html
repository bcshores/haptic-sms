Privacy Policy: Brent Shores Personal Assistant SMS

Effective October 11, 2026

Brent Shores ("we") operates a personal assistant text-messaging service for himself and a small number of family members and friends. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number and your name, provided when you are added as a contact or when you text the number.
  • The content of text messages you send to and receive from the service, and the dates and times of those messages.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your messages, send you messages you agreed to receive, and pass messages along to Brent Shores. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information is processed only by the service providers needed to deliver messages (for example, our SMS carrier provider, Twilio) and is never sold or rented.

Retention

Message history is kept only as long as needed to run the service, and is deleted on request.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. To have your information deleted, email hapticbrent@gmail.com.
