SMS Terms and Conditions: Brent Shores Personal Assistant

Effective October 11, 2026

Program description

A personal assistant text-messaging service run by Brent Shores, an individual. It sends replies, reminders and alerts to Brent and to family members and friends who have agreed to receive messages, and answers their questions. It sends no marketing messages.

How you opt in

You receive messages only if you (1) texted the assistant's number first, or (2) gave Brent Shores your verbal consent, in person or by phone, to be added. Consent is never requested by text message. See Opt-in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per week.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to cancel at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email hapticbrent@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
